## Review programme

### Review request triggers

Request a review only after a genuine service milestone and only where privacy, funding-body and organisational rules allow it. Suitable triggers may include:

- successful onboarding;
- a completed care milestone;
- positive unsolicited feedback;
- a resolved service issue followed by confirmed satisfaction.

### Rules

- Do not offer incentives.
- Do not ask only clients expected to leave positive feedback.
- Never suggest mentioning a diagnosis, funding plan or clinical details.
- Offer a private feedback route alongside the public review route.
- Obtain permission before republishing any review or testimonial.
- Treat reviews as sensitive when they reveal a care relationship.

### Suggested neutral request

> Thank you for trusting Gentle Care. If you would like to share your experience, you can leave an honest review using this link. Please avoid including private health, funding or personal information. If you would prefer to give feedback privately, reply to us directly.
