# Gentle Care Question-Led Content Backlog

**Prepared:** 25 July 2026
**Rule:** Publish only after Gemma validates service, registration, funding and clinical claims.

## Selection method

Prioritise questions that meet all four conditions:

1. Asked by real prospects, families, referrers or coordinators.
2. Connected to a service Gentle Care can genuinely deliver.
3. Answerable with direct, current and source-backed information.
4. Has a clear next step without forcing a sales pitch.

Use GSC queries, GHL enquiry themes, calls, GBP questions, referrer feedback and competitor gaps as evidence. Do not create articles merely to target a keyword.

Build only if GSC, enquiries or referrer conversations demonstrate demand.

1. **DVA Community Nursing referral checklist for Sydney clinicians**
   - Audience: GPs, hospitals and discharge planners.
   - Include official sources and a revision date.
   - Do not duplicate the commercial DVA page.

2. **Preparing for an in-home nursing assessment**
   - Audience: families and clients.
   - Cover documents, medication lists, consent and home access without collecting information in the article.

3. **NDIS community nursing versus daily personal activities**
   - Audience: participants, families and support coordinators.
   - Use careful “may” and “depends on the plan” language.

4. **Hospital discharge to home: a Sydney family checklist**
   - Audience: families and discharge teams.
   - Include red-flag advice directing urgent issues to emergency services or treating clinicians.

5. **Questions to ask an in-home nursing provider**
   - Audience: comparison-stage families.
   - Focus on clinical governance, continuity, escalation, consent and communication.

6. **Support at Home nursing: what to confirm before choosing a provider**
   - Publish only after policy and programme wording is checked against current government sources.

## Priority 3: local evidence

Before expanding location content, collect real proof for each region:

- actual active service boundary;
- staff coverage or travel capability;
- realistic intake availability;
- local hospitals/referral pathways, without implying partnerships;
- unique service demand and page questions;
- verified reviews or stories with consent.

Merge, improve or noindex location pages that cannot be made independently useful. Avoid doorway-page expansion.

## Page answer format for AEO/GEO

For each priority question:

1. Give a direct 40 to 80 word answer under a descriptive heading.
2. State who the answer applies to.
3. State material caveats.
4. Name the next step.
5. Link to the primary official source when discussing funding or eligibility.
6. Add a visible reviewed date when information can change.
7. Keep the same facts consistent in HTML, schema, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt` and `ai-index.json`.

## Editorial quality gate

Do not publish if any answer contains:

- unsupported “best”, “leading” or guaranteed outcome claims;
- guaranteed eligibility, funding, response or availability;
- an unverified registration/accreditation claim;
- copied competitor wording;
- medical advice that should come from a treating clinician;
- a fabricated review, patient story, partner or staff credential;
- duplicate local content with only a suburb name changed.

## Monthly feedback loop

1. Export GSC questions and low-CTR high-impression queries.
2. Aggregate GHL enquiry themes without PII.
3. Add new questions from calls and referrer conversations.
4. Score each question on demand, commercial relevance, evidence availability and risk.
5. Improve an existing page before creating a new one.
6. Track whether the page gained impressions, clicks, citations and qualified enquiries.
